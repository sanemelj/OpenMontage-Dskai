## Music and SFX plan (free-only)

### Music direction
Use a dark, rhythmic, modern noir / electronic score with a steady pulse. The music should be sparse early, rise quickly during the chase, and resolve only at the final release.

Recommended free-only sources:
- YouTube Audio Library (search: dark cinematic, urban noir, suspense, tension, pulse)
- Free Music Archive (search: cinematic minimal, dark electronic, nocturnal city)
- Pixabay music library (if available and licensed for use)

Suggested mood progression:
- 0:00–1:20: near-silence, rain and ambience only
- 1:20–2:40: low pulse, dark synths, tension strings
- 2:40–5:00: full chase pulse with cinematic drums and tension rise
- 5:00–7:00: resolve tone, less dense, final payoff
- 7:00–8:30: final silence and isolated ambience

### SFX plan
Keep sound design as a core storytelling device.

Priority effects:
- rain / city ambience / wet pavement
- distant sirens
- footsteps sync with cuts
- alley echoes
- metal/clang / gate slam
- traffic hum / distant train / tunnel resonance
- low drone / ominous hum
- door slam or emergency gate noise
- glass / reflective surfaces / impact hits

Recommended free-only sources:
- Freesound.org
- YouTube Audio Library
- Pixabay sound effects
- Wikimedia Commons audio clips where rights allow

### Mixing approach
- Build a stereo mix with layered ambience first
- Add music under the scene, ducking it where SFX must lead
- Keep low frequencies controlled so the city feel remains crisp
- Use dynamic fades instead of flat background audio
