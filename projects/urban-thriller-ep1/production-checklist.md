# Urban Thriller Episode 1 — Production Checklist

## Target audience
- viewers who like tense, atmospheric action
- city-noir, chase, survival, and motion-led storytelling
- people who watch short cinematic videos without dialogue

## Best hook
"A city that should be empty... but isn't."

## Best title
"Midnight Run"

## Best CTA at end
Subscribe for more cinematic city stories.

## Content notes
- Keep visuals clean and understandable
- Let the sound design carry emotional beats
- Avoid long explanations or text blocks
- Use the last frame to leave a stronger memory
