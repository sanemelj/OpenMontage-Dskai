Wide cinematic establishing shot, night, light rain, a pleasant middle-class residential street in Delmas, Port-au-Prince, Haiti: well-kept two-storey houses painted in pastel yellow, turquoise and coral, decorative iron gates, bougainvillea spilling over walls, a few palm trees, warm sodium streetlights, wet asphalt reflecting light. In the middle of the street one single unfinished grey concrete house with plastic over its windows stands out, a white police pickup truck with flashing red and blue lights parked in front of it. Anamorphic 2.39:1 framing, Kodak Vision3 500T film look, no people. Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke anamorphic lens, photorealistic.
